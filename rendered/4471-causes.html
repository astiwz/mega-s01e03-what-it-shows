How the desk should use this split

The subject-only merge rule is not reliable for these reports. The existing classifier and triage explorer compare causes instead: the same subject can describe different problems, and different subjects can describe the same problem.

  • A booking-v3 form report from alder.physio and an app report from the same tenant belong to different causes, even when both say "Booking on the wrong date".
  • Booking-v3 form reports from pale.harbour and alder.physio share the hint cause, even when their subjects differ.
  • Without tenant metadata, the cause is unknown. Do not merge on a matching subject alone; first establish the tenant, locale, form version, and channel.

The accepted historical decision 0001 is preserved as evidence of the old rule. This page documents the contrary evidence for #4471; it does not claim that the desk has accepted a replacement policy.
